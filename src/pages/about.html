About PakExamPrep

Free, checked notes and MCQs for Pakistani government job tests.

Who writes it

PakExamPrep is run by a Geography graduate with a BS (Hons) and an MS in Geography and experience teaching home-tuition students. The writer prepares for the same FPSC and AJK PSC tests the site covers, so every page is written from the candidate's side of the desk.

How pages are checked

  • Every answer is checked against standard textbooks before a page goes live.
  • Where sources give different figures, such as glacier lengths or peak heights, the page says "about" and uses one source consistently.
  • Pages show the date they were last updated, and anything that changes, such as office holders, is rechecked before each exam season.

Spotted a mistake?

Wrong answers cost candidates marks, so corrections come first. Send the page and the question number through the contact page and it will be fixed, usually within a day.

Please note

PakExamPrep is an independent practice site. It is not linked to FPSC, AJK PSC or any other commission. Always confirm dates, eligibility and syllabus in the official notice.
