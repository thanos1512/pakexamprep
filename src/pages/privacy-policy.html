Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

What we collect

PakExamPrep has no accounts or sign-up forms. Your quiz answers stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) to run and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it sends the font files.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads through Google AdSense to keep the site free. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings, and read how Google uses data in its partner-sites policy.

If we add analytics in future, this page will be updated to say which service is used and what it collects.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. We do not share it or add it to any mailing list.

Children

The site is written for adults preparing for job tests and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
